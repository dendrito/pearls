# French citations: Sefaria check (27 Nov 2026)

All 7,780 distinct refs the citation linker finds in the 180 French chapters were fetched from Sefaria (API v3).

- 7,721 open with text (7,574 first pass, plus 147 that the first pass could not reach and a second pass opened, except 3 below).
- 3 are not in Sefaria: Tosefta Sotah 7:17, Tosefta Sheviit 5:20, Kaf HaChaim 16:32.
- 52 return "no text" (Sefaria lacks the work or the number): Tosefta (Sheviit, Menachot, Avodah Zarah, Mikvaot, Yadayim, Bava Metzia), many Kaf HaChaim, Taz and Shakh numbers beyond Sefaria's segmentation, Pitchei Teshuva, Arukh HaShulchan YD 153 and 216, Sforno on Devarim 22:6, Ramban on Shemot 20:1, Esther and Bamidbar Rabbah numbering, Malbim, Darkhei Moshe, Prisha ḤM 262:21, Yerushalmi Moed Katan 10:7, Peninei Halakha Kashrut 38 and Zemanim 14:11. The app leaves such citations unlinked (BOUNDS), as in the other books.
- 7 returned a whole work. Six were three-part locations written as `n:m:k` (SA 181:1:10, 175:2:6, YD 240:17:24, 39:11:13, 39:4:18, 48:1:6); fixed to `n:m, k`. One (Beit Yosef YD 201) is a siman with no se'if, which Sefaria serves whole.
- One misread ("Shulḥan Arukh, ibid. 316:7" read as OḤ 328:316:7) is now "Shulḥan Arukh OḤ 316:7".
- The tractate Ḥullin was written four ways (Houlin, 'Houlin, Houllin, Chullin); now Ḥullin everywhere.
