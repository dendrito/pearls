# French translation: what was settled, and where the Hebrew itself looks off (27 Nov 2026)

## Settled from Har Bracha's own French and the brief
- **téfillines** (the brief; 861 uses, one form everywhere; the plural is feminine, as the AI already agreed it).
- **Cohen / cohen / Cohanim** (Har Bracha's French: "un cohen", "Torat Cohanim", "Sifté Cohen"). Titles such as Mishpat Kohen,
  Da'at Kohen and names such as Hakohen keep their own spelling.
- **Ḥullin** for the tractate (was Houlin, 'Houlin, Houllin, Chullin).
- Everything else already followed the 22 Nov measured glossary (Dieu, bénédiction, décisionnaires, nos sages, Chabbat in prose,
  Qidouch, kazaït, Mézonot; citations in house forms).

## The 35 "[?]" marks
All were on references, none on a doubtful word of translation. Each was checked against the Hebrew; the Hebrew is explicit,
so the mark is removed and the text follows the Hebrew. Two were real readings and are now fixed: "(17:11)" is "17, note 11"
(as the English has it), and "Beur Ha-Gra 14" is the Beur Ha-Gra on **YD** (י"ד), not a number. "mûre [?] (asna)" was an
invented gloss: the Hebrew has "פטל ואסנה", now "la framboise (petel) et l'asna".

## Where the Hebrew (Har Bracha's text) seems to cite the wrong place, kept as written
Worth a note to Har Bracha; we do not correct their references silently.
- 11-01: Devarim 6:13 for "Chacun sa mère et son père craindra" (that is Vayikra 19:3); Shemot 20:11 and 20:12 for the
  honouring of parents and "Tu ne tueras pas" (20:12 and 20:13); Devarim 5:15 for honouring parents (5:16).
- 11-07: Vayikra 19:1 for "Vous serez saints" (19:2).
- 11-08: Bereshit 33:25 for the third day after circumcision (34:25).
- 17-05 note 11: Devarim 21:11-12 for shaatnez (22:11-12).
- 17-18: "SA 24:15-8", a range that runs backwards (probably 15-18).
- 11-05: "Yerushalmi Mo'ed Katan 10:7": that tractate has only three chapters on Sefaria.
- 10-15: the sentence about Mount Tabor ends "il a un aspect […]": the Hebrew itself breaks off before footnote 1.
