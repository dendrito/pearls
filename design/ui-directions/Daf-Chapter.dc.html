1. Zakhor and Shamor א. זכור ושמור

As we have seen above (1:8), there are two crucial mitzvot that form the backbone of Shabbat: Zakhor and Shamor.

Shamor instructs us to refrain from all labor. In this way we clear space in our soul, which we are commanded to fill with positive content.
