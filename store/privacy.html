Pearls privacy policy

Pearls (Android app app.pearls.halakha) · Effective 30 October 2026

In short: Pearls doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Pearls keeps the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • your listening and reading progress, streaks and study plan;
  • your playlists, liked chapters, saved passages and notes;
  • your settings (skin, language, typeface and so on);
  • chapters you download, and Hebrew text saved for offline reading.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Connections the app makes

To show text and play recordings, Pearls connects to the publisher's public websites, the same way a web browser would:

AddressWhat for
ph.yhb.org.ilHebrew text of Peninei Halakha, when you open it
cdn1.yhb.org.ilThe audio recordings, when you play or download a chapter

These requests carry no information about you beyond what any internet connection reveals: your device's IP address and a technical "user agent" string. Those sites belong to Yeshivat Har Bracha, which has its own privacy policy. Downloads are made through Android's built-in download manager.

Links to other sites, such as Sefaria and Meldar, open in your web browser only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load text and audio, and to know when you're offline or on Wi‑Fi (for "Wi‑Fi only" downloads).
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: so audio keeps playing with the screen off, with playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

Pearls doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Pearls isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Content

The texts and recordings are Peninei Halakha by Rabbi Eliezer Melamed, © Yeshivat Har Bracha, licensed CC BY 4.0. Some volumes have no official English edition. Their English in Pearls is an AI translation, labelled as such throughout the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Pearls ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before the app does.
