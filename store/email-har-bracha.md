**Subject:** Permission to publish "Pearls", a free Peninei Halakha study app, on Google Play

Dear Har Bracha Publications team,

I've built a free study app for Peninei Halakha in English, and before publishing it on Google Play I'd like to ask
your permission and blessing.

I've seen your new Peninei Halakha app on iPhone (mazal tov!), and I don't want to compete with it or confuse your
readers. Pearls grew out of my own study: it's audio-first, works offline, and offers transliteration choices,
including Spanish & Portuguese spelling. If you'd prefer, I'd gladly put any of this at your disposal for your Android
launch, link to your app from Pearls, or rename Pearls so it can't be mistaken for yours.

The app, called Pearls, lets people read the official English translation and listen to Rav Melamed's recordings,
with playlists, a daily study plan, and offline downloads. It credits Rabbi Eliezer Melamed and Yeshivat Har Bracha
on the home screen and on a full credits page, links back to ph.yhb.org.il for every section, and follows the CC BY
4.0 terms on your site. It's free, has no ads, collects no data, and will never be sold.

I have three questions:

1. **Publishing.** May I publish it on Google Play under the name "Pearls: Peninei Halakha"? If you prefer the name
   not to appear, I'll use "Pearls: Halakha in English".
2. **Recordings.** Your terms put the site's content under CC BY 4.0. Does that include the audio on
   cdn1.yhb.org.il? The app streams it, and lets users download chapters to their phone for offline listening.
3. **AI translations.** For the volumes with no English edition (Berakhot, Kashrut, Family Purity, Giyur, Emuna), the
   app offers an AI translation of the Hebrew. It is labelled as unofficial and unreviewed on every page, with the
   Hebrew one tap away. Are you comfortable with this, or would you prefer I remove it or show only the Hebrew? If
   your translators would ever like to review or correct it, I'd gladly incorporate their changes.

I'm happy to send the app for you to try, change anything you'd like, and share the store link when it's live.

Thank you for making this Torah so freely available.

With respect and gratitude,
[Your name]
